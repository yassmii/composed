# Explore a visual direction

Use when identity or composition is materially open. Skip for a settled direction or local refinement.

## Ground the exploration

Start from the real content, audience's situation, product mechanism, assets and constraints. Identify
the category's usual composition and its fashionable opposite; neither is automatically wrong, but
neither is a sufficient rationale. Look beyond competitor websites: publications, signage, notation,
objects, places or practices can suggest an information structure, rhythm or material treatment.

Inspect supplied references when tools permit. Record source, what was actually seen, the transferable
principle and what not to imitate. If a reference cannot be inspected, label it a hypothesis. Do not
invent reference observations, copy a signature composition, or imply permission to reuse its assets.
Use existing product material when browsing or image generation is unavailable.

## Create alternatives that differ structurally

For an open substantial surface, two or three viable directions are usually enough. Give each a
different organising idea, not the same template in different colours. Vary content arrangement,
information relationships, imagery or interaction where the task permits; respect pinned dimensions.
Every option must support the same factual content, primary task and practical constraints.

Describe each option concretely: rationale tied to the audience; opening composition and progression;
type and colour roles; image treatment; a recognisable detail or interaction; narrow-screen adaptation;
asset needs and a real trade-off. State exact proposed tokens when resolving the chosen direction,
not a list of taste adjectives. Familiar patterns can remain when they serve the task.

Example, not a preset: a lesson-planning tool could foreground an editable sequence, a comparison of
objectives and activities, or a schedule organised around classroom time. These are different ways
to expose the work. Adding a chalk font to the same three-card hero is not another concept. Neither
the subject nor its physical artifacts require skeuomorphic decoration.

## Select and preserve intent

Present comparable sketches or samples if useful, using equal content and fidelity. Judge relevance,
task clarity, identity and feasibility. Recommend a direction and explain the strongest reason not to
choose it. Respect an already authorised choice or explicit delegation; do not manufacture repeated
confirmation steps. An unresolved material choice belongs with the user before dependent implementation.

If options converge on the same skeleton, revisit their organising ideas before adding decoration.
Change one meaningful axis to explore further; random selection alone is not design judgment. Consult
past decisions where available to avoid repeating the same default, without a global history tracker
or rejecting an existing brand simply because it was used before.

Once selected, record the rationale and concrete decisions in the existing design.md, linked to
canonical product/brand sources. Keep relevant rejected options and why. During implementation,
compare the actual structure to that intent; a distinctive proposal that becomes a generic template
has not been carried through. Do not declare aesthetic or conversion gains without observation.
