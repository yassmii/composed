# Research — collaborative workspace

[Version française](README.fr.md)

This research draws on Notion and Airtable for the functional surface, then on AppFlowy, AFFiNE, Docmost, Anytype and Any for the requirements of software freedom, self-hosting, local-first operation, collaboration and performance.

The important result is not to copy these products. AppFlowy and Docmost demonstrate AGPL cores, AFFiNE demonstrates the value of documents + canvas + tables but also the risk of an Enterprise boundary, and Anytype demonstrates that a "source-available" product does not by itself meet the definition of a fully free product. Any serves as a reference for a local, encrypted, convergent building block, not as a finished product.

The sources are deliberately narrow and attributable. They document observable behaviours; design choices specific to the contract are explicitly marked in `../contracts/traceability.yaml`.

Help pages are living resources. Content hashes let `npm run verify:research` flag drift before the contract is promoted.
